# Agent Trust Lab

Open research on GEO, Agent Trust, and AI discovery mechanisms.

An open research initiative by CHAIN GEO.

## The Research Question

As AI agents become autonomous participants in the digital economy, how do they decide which entities, sources, services, and protocols to trust?

## Agent Trust Selection Hypothesis (ATSH)

Given multiple entities capable of fulfilling the same task, AI agents may exhibit systematic preferences toward entities that are familiar, independently endorsed, verifiable, and supported by successful interaction histories.

Read the [ATSH v0.1 research note](research/hypothesis.md) and the [methodology](research/methodology.md). Start with the [H01 familiarity pilot](experiments/H01-familiarity/README.md).

## Our Mission

To measure, understand, and verify the mechanisms that influence autonomous agent selection.

We aim to develop open benchmarks, reproducible experiments, transparent methodologies, and trusted intelligence infrastructure for the Agent economy.

## Research Principles

- Evidence over hype.
- Reproducibility over speculation.
- Verification over popularity.
- Transparency over black-box scoring.
- Trustworthiness is not equivalent to familiarity.

## Project Status

Early-stage research. The hypotheses presented in this repository have not yet been validated. Experimental results, including negative findings, will be published transparently.

CHAIN GEO Research retains control over research design, interpretation, and publication. Commercial relationships relevant to a study will be disclosed alongside that study.
