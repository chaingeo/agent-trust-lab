# Literature Review

This initial reading list motivates controls for the pilot. Direct evidence for ATSH awaits dedicated experiments.

- Zajonc (1968), [Attitudinal effects of mere exposure](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0025848), studies human responses to prior exposure and motivates H01's intervention.
- Zheng et al. (2023), [Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685), describes position bias in comparative judgments and motivates balanced candidate ordering.
